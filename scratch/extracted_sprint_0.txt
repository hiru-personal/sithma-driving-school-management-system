1. Introduction 
 
1.1Problem Specification 
Sithma Driving School currently operates across three branches Maharagama, Werahara, 
and Delgoda and manages its entire operation manually. At present, the school does not 
use any dedicated software system; student registrations, lesson bookings, license 
progress, and payment records are all maintained using paper-based records and informal, 
phone-based communication between students, instructors, and office staff. 
At each branch, a Data Entry Officer/Staff member is responsible for registering students, 
manually recording dates received from the Department of Motor Traffic (DMT)  such as 
medical examination, learner's registration, and learner's exam dates verifying payment 
slips, and managing lesson bookings, all without any centralized system to support these 
tasks. 
This manual approach creates several significant problems: 
• Fragmented, inconsistent records - student information, DMT dates, and 
payment records are kept separately at each branch, with no consolidated or real-
time view of a student's overall progress. 
• High risk of missed regulatory deadlines - because a student must pass the Trial 
(practical driving test) within 1.5 years of passing the learner's exam, and is 
limited to a maximum of 3 Trial attempts, any date that is missed, delayed, or 
incorrectly recorded manually can cause a student to lose an attempt or miss their 
licensing window entirely. 
• Manual, error-prone payment verification - payments are made outside the 
system (e.g., bank transfer), and staff must manually cross-check 
uploaded/physical payment slips against bookings, which is slow and susceptible 
to human error. 

=== PAGE 4 ===

• No self-service access for students - students have no way to check their own 
registration status, DMT dates, remaining lessons, or package details without 
directly contacting staff, creating unnecessary dependency and communication 
overhead. 
• No structured exam preparation tool - students preparing for the DMT written 
exam have no formal, school-provided practice resource and must rely on external 
materials. 
• Administrative overload - a single Data Entry Officer per branch is responsible 
for registration, date tracking, payment verification, and booking management, 
creating a bottleneck as student volume grows. 
Together, these issues limit Sithma Driving School's ability to scale, increase the risk of 
compliance failures with DMT timelines, and result in a poor, opaque experience for 
students who have no visibility into their own progress. 
1.2 Business idea 
The core idea is to replace Sithma Driving School's fully manual, paper-based operation 
with a single, centralized Sithma Driving School Management System.A digital platform 
that brings student registration, DMT milestone and Trial tracking, vehicle package and 
lesson management, time-slot booking, payment verification, notifications, and exam 
practice together in one place, accessible to students, staff, and instructors across all three 
branches. 
For the business, this creates real, tangible value. It removes the administrative bottleneck 
currently placed on a single Data Entry Officer per branch by automating record-keeping, 
date tracking, and payment cross-checking. It significantly reduces the risk of a student 
missing a critical DMT deadline (such as the 1.5-year Trial window or the 3-attempt 
limit) by tracking these rules automatically instead of relying on manual memory and 
paperwork. It gives branch and school management a consolidated, real-time view of 
operations - registrations, bookings, payments, and student progress - across all three 
branches for the first time, something that is simply not possible today. And for students, 
it turns a slow, staff-dependent process into a transparent, self-service experience: they 
can check their own dates, remaining lessons, and package status anytime, receive 
automatic notifications the moment something changes, and prepare for their DMT 
written exam through a built-in multilingual quiz module. In short, this system is the 
digital backbone that lets Sithma Driving School scale its operations, reduce compliance 
risk, and deliver a modern, trustworthy experience - something a manual, three-branch 
paper system cannot support as the business grows. 
 
 

=== PAGE 5 ===

1.3 Solution Outline 
The proposed solution is a centralized, web and mobile-accessible Sithma Driving School 
Management System built around the four core Epics identified for this project: (1) 
Student Registration, DMT Milestone Tracking, Student/Staff Profile & Vehicle 
Package Management - capturing Type 1/Type 2 student registrations, tracking DMT 
medical/registration/exam/trial dates, managing profiles, and handling package selection 
(Car, Bike, Three-Wheeler, Heavy Vehicle); (2) Lesson & Time-Slot Management - 
allowing staff to manage branch/instructor availability and students to book lesson slots 
and free weekly theory/practical classes; (3) Payment Management & Verification - 
where students upload payment slips for staff to manually verify, paired with an in-app 
notification service that alerts students and staff whenever a registration, date, or payment 
status changes; and (4) Exam Management - a multilingual (Sinhala, Tamil, English) 
quiz module covering both Light and Heavy Vehicle question sets, with answers and 
marks handled directly through the system. 
The system will be built using a React front end, a Node.js/Express REST API back 
end, and a MySQL database, deployed on a cloud-hosted platform accessible to students, 
staff, and instructors across all three branches. The high-level architecture is shown 
below.

1.4 Key Benefits 
 
• Time saved: -  
 
Registration, DMT date tracking, lesson booking, and payment checks all happen in one 
centralized system instead of being handled manually across three separate branches, 
cutting down significantly on administrative time per student.  
 
• Cost reduction: -  
 
Automating record-keeping and payment verification reduces the staffing overhead 
currently needed to manage paper-based processes, allowing the same office staff to 
support a larger number of students without additional hires.  
 
• Error reduction: - 
 
Automated enforcement of the 3-Trial-attempt limit and the 1.5-year Trial deadline 
removes the risk of human error in manually tracking these DMT rules, reducing the 
chance of a student losing an attempt or missing their licensing window due to a missed 
or miscalculated date.  
 
• Fewer missed communications: - 
 
Automatic in-app notifications ensure students and staff are informed the moment a 
registration, DMT date, or payment status changes, removing dependency on staff 
remembering to call or message students.  
 
• Better decision-making via reports: -  
 
Branch and school management gain a consolidated, real-time view of registrations, 
bookings, payments, and student progress across all three branches - visibility that does 
not exist today - supporting more informed decisions on staffing, scheduling, and 
package pricing. 
  
• Scalability: -  
 
A centralized digital system allows Sithma Driving School to handle more students and 
more course types (Light and Heavy Vehicle, refresher, standalone lessons) without a 
proportional increase in administrative effort, supporting business growth.  
 
• Improved customer (student) experience: - 
 
Students gain self-service visibility into their own registration status, DMT dates, 
remaining lessons, and package details at any time, plus access to multilingual

• Faster, more reliable payment verification: -

transfer cross-checking, speeding up payment confirmation and reducing disputes.  
 
• Consistency across branches: -  
All three branches (Maharagama, Werahara, Delgoda) operate on the same system and 
rules, ensuring students receive a consistent experience and staff apply DMT timeline 
rules uniformly.

2. Objectives 
 
2.1 Main Objective 
To design and develop, within the 15-week project timeline, a centralized web-based 
management system for Sithma Driving School that digitizes student registration, DMT 
milestone and Trial tracking, lesson and time-slot booking, payment verification, and 
multilingual exam management across all three branches, replacing the current fully 
manual, paper-based process 
(This is written as a SMART objective — Specific (names the exact system and its four 
core functions), Measurable (a working system covering registration, DMT tracking, 
booking, payment, and exam management), Achievable (scoped to what the team can 
realistically build), Relevant (directly addresses the problems identified in 1.1), and 
Time-bound (within the 15-week project timeline).) 
 
2.2. Sub Objectives 
 
▪ To implement a Student Registration, DMT Milestone Tracking, and Profile 
& Vehicle Package Management module that supports both Type 1 (new 
learner) and Type 2 (trial-ready) student categories, tracks 
medical/registration/exam/Trial dates, and automatically enforces the 3-attempt 
and 1.5-year Trial rules, by the end of the development phase.  
 
▪ To implement a Lesson & Time-Slot Management module that allows staff to 
manage branch and instructor availability and allows students to book lesson slots 
across Light Vehicle (Car, Bike, Three-Wheeler) and Heavy Vehicle (Bus) 
categories, as well as free weekly theory/practical classes, within the project's 
development sprints.  
 
▪ To implement a Payment Management & Verification module, including in-app 
notifications, that allows students to upload payment slips and staff to manually 
verify them against bookings, reducing payment confirmation time and 
communication delays.  
 
▪ To implement a multilingual (Sinhala, Tamil, English) Exam Management 
module offering quiz-style practice questions for both Light and Heavy Vehicle 
DMT written exams, with marks handled directly through the system, to give 
students a structured, self-service exam preparation tool.

9. Definition of Done (DoD) 
 
A user story is considered "Done" when: 
• The feature has been fully implemented according to the acceptance criteria of the 
user story. 
• The code has been reviewed by at least one other team member (peer code 
review). 
• The feature has been tested (unit/functional testing) and works without critical 
bugs. 
• The UI (if applicable) is functional, responsive, and consistent with the rest of the 
system's design. 
• The feature has been integrated into the main branch/build without breaking 
existing functionality. 
• The feature has been tested on the intended platform/device without major issues. 
• Relevant documentation (e.g., code comments, user guide notes) has been updated 
if needed. 
• The feature has been demonstrated to and accepted by the team/supervisor (or 
Product Owner, if applicable). 
• No known high-priority bugs remain open for that story.
